# SOP-10 Discharge consent exceedance response and reporting

Area: 500 Discharge. Tags: AIT-501, AIT-502, AIT-401, TT-501, FIT-501.

## 10.1 Purpose and scope
The mill's discharge consent sets limits on the treated effluent: pH 6.5 to 8.5, clarified turbidity at most
20 NTU, COD at most 250 mg/L and temperature at most 38 degC. This SOP covers what to do when a discharge
parameter exceeds its limit, and the records the consent requires.

## 10.2 Symptoms and alarms
- Critical alarms: discharge pH above or below consent (AIT-501 HIHI or LOLO), clarified turbidity above consent
  (AIT-401 HIHI), discharge COD above consent (AIT-502 HIHI), discharge temperature above consent (TT-501 HIHI).
- Pre-alarms at the HI and LO limits give time to act before the consent is breached.

## 10.3 Likely causes
- Any upstream upset: shock load (SOP-01), pH control failure (SOP-02, SOP-03), coagulation failure (SOP-04,
  SOP-05, SOP-09), aeration failure (SOP-06) or high temperature (SOP-12).

## 10.4 Immediate checks
- Confirm the reading with a grab sample analysed in the laboratory.
- Identify the upstream cause from the incident list and the trends.
- Check the free volume in the equalisation tank for holding back influent.

## 10.5 Corrective actions
- Remove the cause using the relevant SOP.
- With the supervisor's authorisation, reduce or stop the forward flow and hold effluent in the equalisation tank
  until the discharge is back within consent.
- Keep the grab samples (labelled and refrigerated) for the records.

## 10.6 Escalation and records
- Any consent exceedance must be reported to the environmental officer the same day.
- The daily compliance report records the time out of consent for each parameter, the peak values and the
  incident references; add the cause and the actions in the comments.
