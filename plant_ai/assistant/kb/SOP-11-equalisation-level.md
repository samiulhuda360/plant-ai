# SOP-11 Equalisation tank high or low level

Area: 100 Equalisation. Tags: LIT-101, FV-101, ZT-101, FIT-101, FIT-102.

## 11.1 Purpose and scope
The equalisation tank evens out the flow and the strength of the dye-house effluent. Its level controller
adjusts the forward flow valve FV-101 to keep the level near its setpoint while leaving room for dye dumps.
High level risks an overflow to the stormwater drain; low level risks running the forward flow pumps dry.

## 11.2 Symptoms and alarms
- Equalisation tank level high (LIT-101 HI) or very high, with overflow risk (LIT-101 HIHI).
- Equalisation tank level low (LIT-101 LO).

## 11.3 Likely causes
- Inflow above the treatment capacity: shock loads or several dumps together (SOP-01).
- The forward flow valve not opening (SOP-07) or a forward pump trip.
- Level transmitter fault.

## 11.4 Immediate checks
- Compare the influent flow FIT-101 with the forward flow FIT-102.
- Check that the valve position ZT-101 follows the command FV-101.
- Check the level locally on the tank gauge.

## 11.5 Corrective actions
- Ask the dye-house to hold drains while the level is high.
- If the valve is stuck, follow SOP-07.
- The forward flow may only be raised above the design flow with the supervisor's agreement, because the
  clarifier turbidity rises at high flow (SOP-09).

## 11.6 Escalation and records
- Any overflow must be reported to the environmental officer immediately.
- Record the peak level and the cause.
