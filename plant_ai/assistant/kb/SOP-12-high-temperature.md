# SOP-12 High temperature effluent

Area: 100 and 500. Tags: TT-101, TT-501, AIT-301.

## 12.1 Purpose and scope
Dyeing runs at 60 to 130 degC, so hot baths reach the drain. High temperature lowers the oxygen the aeration
basin can hold, stresses the biology, and the consent limits the discharge temperature to 38 degC.

## 12.2 Symptoms and alarms
- Influent temperature high (TT-101 HI).
- Discharge temperature high (TT-501 HI), above consent (TT-501 HIHI).
- Dissolved oxygen harder to hold with the blower at high speed.

## 12.3 Likely causes
- Hot dye baths or rinses drained without passing the heat-recovery exchanger.
- Heat-recovery exchanger bypassed or fouled.
- Hot weather combined with a high dyeing load.

## 12.4 Immediate checks
- Check the heat-recovery exchanger outlet temperature and its bypass valve.
- Ask the dye-house which machines are draining hot.
- Check the blower speed and the dissolved oxygen trend.

## 12.5 Corrective actions
- Ask the dye-house to drain hot baths through the heat-recovery exchanger and to stagger hot drains.
- Use the equalisation storage to hold back hot effluent so it can cool and mix.

## 12.6 Escalation and records
- If TT-501 exceeds 38 degC, follow SOP-10.
- Record the peak temperatures and the cause.
