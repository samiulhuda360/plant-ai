# SOP-01 Influent shock load (dye batch dump)

Area: 100 Influent and equalisation. Tags: FIT-101, AIT-101, TT-101, AIT-102, AIT-103, LIT-101.

## 1.1 Purpose and scope
How to respond when the dye-house releases an abnormal load to drain: a concentrated or exhausted dye bath, a
rinse dropped all at once, or several machines draining together. A shock load arrives as a sudden rise in
influent flow, pH, temperature, conductivity and turbidity, and it reaches the pH correction and coagulation
stages within the equalisation residence time.

## 1.2 Symptoms and alarms
- Influent flow high (FIT-101 HI), influent pH high (AIT-101 HI) or the pH analyser at its range limit.
- Influent temperature high (TT-101 HI), conductivity high (AIT-102 HI), turbidity high (AIT-103 HI).
- Equalisation level rising quickly (LIT-101). Acid pump P-201 driven to its maximum rate.
- The anomaly monitor reports an extreme operating point (T-squared) led by influent tags.
- Later: pH correction tank pH high, clarified turbidity high, discharge pH and COD rising.

## 1.3 Likely causes
- A dye bath or soaping bath drained without the scheduled hold in the dye-house sump.
- Several dyeing machines finishing at the same time.
- Caustic or soda ash spill washed to the drain in the dispensing room.

## 1.4 Immediate checks
- Phone the dye-house supervisor: which machines drained, what recipe, and how many cubic metres.
- Check the equalisation level trend and the free volume left before the high-high level.
- Confirm the acid pump P-201 is running and the acid day tank level is adequate for the extra demand.
- Confirm the coagulant pump P-202 has increased its rate and the coagulant tank is not low.
- Watch the pH correction tank pH (AIT-201 and AIT-201B) and the clarified turbidity (AIT-401) for the next hour.

## 1.5 Corrective actions
- Ask the dye-house to hold further drains until the equalisation level is back in the normal band.
- If the plant is in fixed-rate dosing, ask the control room to switch to adaptive dosing so acid and coagulant
  follow the load.
- If discharge quality is at risk, follow SOP-10 for diversion and notification.
- Do not bypass the equalisation tank to relieve the level.

## 1.6 Escalation and records
- Inform the shift supervisor of any shock load that drives a discharge parameter above its pre-alarm.
- Record the time, the machines involved, the estimated volume and the peak readings in the shift log.
