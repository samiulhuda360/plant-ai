# SOP-15 Safe handling of sulphuric acid and PAC coagulant

Area: 200 Chemical storage and dosing. Tags: LIT-201, LIT-202, P-201, P-202.

## 15.1 Purpose and scope
Safe work on the acid and coagulant systems: day tanks, transfer and dosing pumps, dosing lines and injection
points. 30 % sulphuric acid causes severe burns and reacts violently with water when concentrated; PAC is acidic
and corrosive to the eyes.

## 15.2 Hazards
- Chemical burns from splashes, pressurised lines and leaking fittings.
- Heat and spattering when acid is mixed with water.
- Slips on chemical spills in the bund.

## 15.3 Required protection
- Face shield over safety goggles, acid-resistant gloves and apron, and safety boots.
- Eyewash and safety shower tested and within 10 seconds of the work area.

## 15.4 Isolation before work
- Obtain a permit to work. Stop the pump from the control room and apply lock-out and tag-out at the motor
  isolator.
- Close and tag the suction and discharge valves. Relieve the line pressure through the drain into a container.
- Flush the line with water before breaking any joint.

## 15.5 Spills
- Contain spills in the bund. Neutralise acid with soda ash before clean-up, and test the pH before disposal to
  the treatment plant.

## 15.6 Records
- Record the permit number, the isolation points and the return to service in the shift log.
