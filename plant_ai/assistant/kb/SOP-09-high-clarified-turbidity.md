# SOP-09 High clarified turbidity and poor settling

Area: 400 Clarifier. Tags: AIT-401, P-202_SP, FIT-202, FIT-102.

## 9.1 Purpose and scope
The clarifier settles the chemical floc and biomass. The clarified turbidity AIT-401 is the main indicator of
coagulation performance and must stay under the consent limit of 20 NTU, with a pre-alarm at 15 NTU.

## 9.2 Symptoms and alarms
- Clarified turbidity rising (AIT-401 rising), turbidity high (AIT-401 HI), above consent (AIT-401 HIHI).
- Floating sludge, pin floc, or a rising sludge blanket.

## 9.3 Likely causes
- Coagulant underdosing: pump fault (SOP-04), empty tank (SOP-05), or a load increase (SOP-01).
- pH outside the coagulation range (SOP-02, SOP-03).
- Hydraulic overload: forward flow FIT-102 well above design.
- Sludge not withdrawn often enough, or overdosing that restabilises the colloids.

## 9.4 Immediate checks
- Check that FIT-202 follows P-202_SP and that the coagulant tank is not low.
- Check the pH correction tank pH on both probes.
- Run a jar test on the flash mixer inlet to confirm the right dose.
- Check the sludge blanket depth with the sludge judge.

## 9.5 Corrective actions
- Restore coagulant dosing first; the controller trim will raise the dose automatically in adaptive mode.
- Reduce the forward flow using the equalisation storage if the clarifier is hydraulically overloaded.
- Withdraw sludge if the blanket is high.

## 9.6 Escalation and records
- If AIT-401 exceeds 20 NTU, follow SOP-10.
- Record the jar-test result and the actions taken.
